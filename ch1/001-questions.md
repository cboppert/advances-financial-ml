## 1.5 Econometrics vs ML

- What's the difference?

Econometrics is static linear analysis. I.E. the models do not learn over time, and you're just doing multivariate matrix manipulation

Machine learning allows for non linear analysis which evolves over time

- How would economics and finance benefit from updating their statistical toolkit?

Reality is non linear and as such cannot be modeled with linear functions which miss out on everything which is modeled in a non linear fashion.

So they should update their toolkit to incorporate new methods of modeling which learn over time and can encapsulate more of the of the solution space.

## 1.6 The Brain

Brains are technically a black box.

- What causes ML critics to decry ML as a black box while embracing discretionary thinking?

There is an underlying belief in human rationality which assumes that for every choice made by a professional investor there will be a followable chain of reasoning which can be analyzed and improved upon.

But critics believe that it is impossible to know how a ML model is attaining its results and as such it's possible that you are basing your investment on a bonkers correlation that's not rational and thus that adds risk which can't be quantified easily.

So critics have an inherent distrust of black box systems despite advocating for systems which are reliant on the ultimate black box which is our own minds.
